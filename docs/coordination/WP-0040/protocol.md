# Physical intake and scoring protocol

This packet releases existing-custody inventory and CPU controls only. The independent custodian owns source/rights and blind order; the implementer and non-author review route receive no private material. A later positive inventory requires an explicit coordinator decision on specific sources, a permitted offline reader, device/lease, immutable input identities, budget and commands. A historical OCR installation or past lease alone gives no current authorization.

## Acquisition design contingent on a subsequent grant

At most 24 previously unseen permitted families, one page and table per family with 1–3 body rows, signed amounts, a complete running balance and a printed total. Resolve families and duplicates, rights, schema, sign, anchor provenance, resolution, ordering and selection before OCR. Fix the first eligible families in that order. Two independent transcriptions and adjudication establish printed truth. Custodian and adjudicator alone hold truth and independent auditor reference boxes. Commit digests before scoring. Only authentic first-pass automatic boxes can select reader views. The authoritative page 4 reader settings (padding/clipping, transform, field binding, parser, device, timeout, seed if supported, no retry, warm-up, serial timing boundaries and truth-independent order) must be bound to an actual available source/reader before subsequent execution. Missing views retain failure status and cost incompleteness. Reuse each physical page/view return across arms; preserve and hash original bytes before scoring.

The ceiling is 24 first passes and 216 rereads (8 cell views and one table view per ambiguous page). Every arm inherits a first-pass singleton with zero additional reads. B otherwise withholds. E uses the least-cardinality distance-one set and exact matching. R seeks distance min(3,full distance); F reads every cell separately; T reads one table view and returns values for every mutable cell. R/F/T share radius one and require full distance at least two. E/R break size ties by summed frozen single-cell rectangle areas, then numeric mask. Compare R and F on the same eligible ambiguous pages. Distinguish unique physical acquisition, per-arm logical views, summed pixels, summed serial reader milliseconds, timing distribution and planning/parsing/common first-pass overhead. A missing cost is unavailable. A zero-eligible denominator gives no physical rate or latency distribution.

## Family and attribution records

After unblinding retain a private per-family keyed record with page/crop/truth/reference digests, raw and parsed values, automatic boxes, signed truth and membership, opening domain, first-pass status, distances, ties/selected masks and view/cost failures. Record each arm's own view return, binding/parse status and cell errors, complete compatible set/cardinality and full-ledger correct/wrong/withheld outcome. Keep all admitted families in the correctness denominator; track pre-admission exclusions and unresolved truth separately. F and T each retain constant-cell mismatches, smallest active mismatch, active-only and full counts, and attribution crossed by truth membership. Active-only rescoring reuses the same return with zero extra acquisition. For box audit compare selected mean IoU to the equally weighted mean across all *other* minimum-cardinality eligible sets before area filtering; distinguish missing boxes, degenerate boxes and no alternatives. Protect source and truth in any later public aggregate.

Report C/W/A over N, all paired transitions versus B, full cohort and ambiguous stratum separately. H1 applied separately to R and T requires newly correct recovery on a B-ambiguous case, zero newly wrong and zero lost correct. H2 requires fewer R cell views than F with at least as many correct and no more wrong on the matched stratum. E-only newly wrong outcomes are retained; leakage, a failed in-model control, or a newly wrong R/F/T holds both promotions. An incomplete cohort, no ambiguous pages or missing required cost yields feasibility. For a complete clean cohort, consider R if it passes H1/H2 and is at least as correct, no more wrong and strictly faster than T. Prefer T if it passes H1, is at least as correct, no more wrong and no slower than R. Report a tie explicitly. Otherwise withhold a winner. Declare the event and actual denominator before any zero-event bound; 1−0.05^(1/n) assumes independent representative families, undefined at zero. A table decision gives no posting credit.
