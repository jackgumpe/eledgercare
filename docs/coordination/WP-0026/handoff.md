## Completed now: witness readback, zero GPU

At the [independent evidence revision][archive], I verified the ZIP SHA-256 and all 108 member sizes and hashes. I compared the **uncompressed files themselves** in P1J2H, P1J3H and P2J4H, then recomputed all 56 span means from the four H captures, including the first-call control. Each reconstruction matched the stored FP32 bytes. The two cross-process warm comparisons and the one within-process warm comparison were exact.

| Per later H call, same three pages | Bytes | Result |
|:--|--:|:--|
| 284 × 1,280 BF16 consumed-token states | 727,040 | Identical across all three |
| 14 × 1,280 FP32 pooled span vectors | 71,680 | Identical across all three |
| 284 emitted IDs and EOS, little-endian uint32 | 1,140 | Identical across all three |

**Interpretation.** Each S call and H call at the same ordinal across the two processes produced exactly the same emitted stream on this input. Each mode also produced both historical streams as the ordinal changed. The first-call H trace differed from the later H traces at the first *recorded* consumed-output row. This recorder did not capture the prefill hidden state; the first differing operation is unknown. Stock has no hidden-state recording. No universal non-perturbation claim follows. See the [decision][decision] and [position erratum][erratum].

**Decision:** this is evidence to attempt W0, not a pass for H0 or C1. The experiment repeated one triple. It did not test whether warming on unrelated pages transfers to different pages or a different binder. The original WP-0024 verdict stands.

# From qualified acquisition to a memory verdict

**Carry the source contract intact.** The attached original PGDA blueprint provides the §1 recurrence. The attached [Twin-Binder v2 specification][v2] supplies the masks, QR rank threshold, eligibility floors, resource limits and scoring; the [amendments][amend] and [retained release instructions][contract] specify donor controls and vetoes. Only W0 and its provenance are new. Previously exposed development inputs remain development inputs.

1. **Fit and freeze.** After warmed H0, the applicable development C1 and inherited CPU preflights, fit the two matched external probes on the disjoint training set. Keep equal parameter/epoch budgets; calibrate only on the separate calibration set. Freeze sensor/runner, model weights, thresholds, rules, scorer and blind plan before held-out execution. Keep Baidu weights frozen; no production changes.
2. **Run all six views.** M0 suffix-only; M1 flat gated delta memory; M2 typed saved stack; M3-Zero disabled matrices; M3 learned external stack; M3-Swap matrices exchanged in both directions. Each blind binder starts with fresh state. Only approved sensor features can be reused. Freeze all six predictions, including abstentions, before the custodian opens the scoring key.
3. **Apply C0 without leaking answers.** On the frozen predictions the custodian identifies pairs for which *both* first windows exposed the active owner, unit and complete ordered schema. Score both the whole set and this common subset. C0 floors: at least 18/24 answerable pairs and 12/16 amendment/subtotal pairs, with at least 4/8 in each of those two strata. Missing an observable declaration is an arm failure, never an opportunity to supply it from truth.
4. **Apply safety before magnitude.** Score 32 held-out pairs (64 binders, 12 or 30 pages), by pair, not by row. Preserve every loss, abstention and ambiguity. Any newly silent wrong owner/unit/column/subtotal relative to M2, a clean T1 pair lost relative to M2, silent T1/T4 error or prohibited side-channel retention vetoes advancement. Losing a correct T2/T3 posting to abstention counts against coverage and net gain. Apply all original effect-size and resource gates as written. Report donor-following, receiver retention, abstention and mixed results separately.

**Question being tested.** For twins with identical suffix $x_\star$, let $F$ be the frozen readout, $M$ the saved matrices, $c$ the receiver control state and $R$ its registry. One direction of the intervention is

$$
F(M_A,c_A,R_A,x_\star)\quad\longrightarrow\quad F(M_B,c_A,R_A,x_\star).
$$

Do the answers follow the earlier owner carried by the donor matrices, and does M3 improve on M2 without creating unsafe postings? The archive readback cannot answer this. A positive blind result earns only independent fresh-family replication of the external probe. Otherwise keep M2 if it qualifies under v2, or record failed/inconclusive. End at non-author review and local coordinator disposition; publish failed and partial evidence as well as successes.
