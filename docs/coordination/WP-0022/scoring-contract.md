# Spatial ownership scoring lock

The primary unit is each keyed logical body row in both arms, including each blank or ambiguous row. The paired matrix counts every target once. We also split setup and post-break rows. Raw observations and predictions must have frozen digests before the custodian opens the key. [Assignment](assignment.md) · [Executable scorer](score.py)

- Unchanged source spelling is carried by the final candidate word. Match by its source ID, original line and pixel-derived point rectangle. Count positive rectangle intersections and separately count words whose center lies inside the region with at least half their area there. A box extending from a cell into a designated margin note cannot earn cell ownership. If a recognized word covers more than one printed token, the diagnostic trace records that separately. The custody regions delimit the study's geometry.
- A populated cell passes on one correct unflagged post, no incorrect unflagged post or duplicate, correct table and Amount role, banner references located in the intended printed property and unit, and section-heading references located in the intended printed occurrence. Those pointers distinguish siblings. A line that mixes words from different keyed rows cannot receive full ownership credit. Raw OCR spellings do not decide ownership. A split suffix from the right cell may have the correct owner and an incomplete literal. Among rightly owned values, count literal equality and mismatches separately.
- An ambiguous row passes if its observed amount receives explicit null-scope review and no field posts silently. An empty target passes **blank safety** when the reader makes no unflagged post into the row; report a typed positively represented blank separately. The frozen reader has no such output type. Withholding something unobserved cannot supply positive evidence of a blank. Keep zero intersecting words on populated targets, candidate absent, incidental flags, flags on the amount, margin captures and outside-row events distinct. Sum explicit flags by logical target and separately by event. False postings take priority even when a correct posting exists in that same row.
- For a word in a right-margin note, its key row includes that note's position or its designated note rectangle. A matching string from that position cannot pass. Record captures opposite known populated and empty cells separately. Every out-of-band unflagged event enters the overall survival decision even though it has no keyed target to enter the paired matrix.
- A full survival finding requires successful ownership/safe withholding on every WinRT target, no silent incorrect assignment inside or outside target rows, and confirmation by the independent replay. The matrix is descriptive; a difference between arms cannot by itself identify a memory fault. Loss of a typed header or line aggregation, an incompatible left edge or wrong final candidate must be distinguished from losing a captured parent despite an observable return cue. Diagnose upstream losses explicitly when they prevent testing the memory. Return to the cloud handoff at disposition. The study does not tune any method.
