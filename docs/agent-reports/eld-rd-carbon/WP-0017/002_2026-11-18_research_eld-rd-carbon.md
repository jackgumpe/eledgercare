### 3. Distinction Between Ordinary Free-Run Timings and Teacher-Forced Probe Timings

Stage A1 deliberately records two separate, non-interchangeable clocks per configuration:

- **Phase 1 — Ordinary Free-Run Window Clock (`free/ordinary-timing.json`):** Measures end-to-end `eng.read(batch, free_dir)` with **zero probe hooks** before Phase 2 is entered. It includes vision encoding (`SAM` + `projector`), prompt prefill, free greedy decoding (including `S3` 8-token bursts and `2–5` post-EOS extra forwards when `S3=1`), `Generation.verify` pin checks, and `native_page` block parsing. Crucially, **emitted token count varies across configurations** on `window-037`: `native`/`all_off`/`add_*`/`remove_S2` emitted `396` tokens (`397` forwards; `402` with `S3=1`), `carbon_bf16`/`all_on`/`remove_{S0,S1a,S3}` emitted `391` tokens (`394` forwards; `392` on `remove_S3`), and `remove_S1b`/`remove_S1c` emitted `406` tokens (`410` forwards). Thus `ordinary_seconds` measures true wall-clock task completion on those two 3-page windows—combining kernel rate, sequence-length variation, burst overshoot, and wrapper overhead.
- **Phase 2 — Serial Teacher-Forced Probe Clock (`probe/window-*-probe.json`):** Runs strictly after Phase 1 artifacts are flushed and closed. It executes one visual+text prompt prefill (`prefill_ms`, which includes `StaticDashCache` buffer initialization when `S2=1`) followed by **strictly serial `q_len == 1` single-token forwards** (`total_forwarded_decode_tokens = 427` on `019` [`417` prefix + `2×5` branch steps] and `50` on `037` [`40` prefix + `2×5` branch steps]), branching via `clone_kv_cache` at the pre-space boundary. Phase 2 **never** uses a multi-token prompt-prefill shortcut (which would route decode tokens through `S1a`/prefill attention instead of `S1b`/`S2` decode kernels) and **never** uses `S3` 8-step bursts, though when `S3=1` it executes `DeviceProbeGuards` per step. Consequently, Phase 2 `decode_ms_per_token` isolates fixed-length `q_len == 1` per-token decode latency (`~107.1–108.1 ms/tok` with `S1b=0` vs `~93.0–96.9 ms/tok` with `S1b=1`), explaining why `S1b` accounts for virtually all of full Carbon's two-window decode speedup.

## Risks, disagreements, and unfinished work

1. **Tied-logit `torch.topk` vs. `torch.argmax` ordering on `14/73` probe passes (measured reporting limitation):** As audited in coordinator commit [`458a9c66896dbf9ac26dfbb08f73cb48d7edacce`](https://github.com/jackgumpe/eledgercare/blob/458a9c66896dbf9ac26dfbb08f73cb48d7edacce/docs/coordination/WP-0017/a1-r1-probe-ties.json), PyTorch `torch.topk(z, k=5)` does not order exact BF16/FP32 logit ties by lowest token ID, whereas greedy generation (`torch.argmax`) deterministically selects the lowest token index. Across 73 Stage A1 probe passes, 35 had tied top-1/top-2 logits (`margin_top1_top2_fp32 == 0.0`, `guarded_tie_at_top1 == true`), and in 14 passes on `window-037` (where tokens `26` `'8'` and `1892` `'80'` tied at `21.625`, e.g. `add_S1c`, `remove_S2`, and `forced_native` passes of `carbon_bf16`/`all_on`/`remove_{S0,S1a,S1b,S1c,S3}`), `guarded_top5[0]` listed `1892` first while [span_probe.py](../../../research/WP-0017/engine/span_probe.py)'s separately recorded `guarded_greedy_argmax_token_id` accurately recorded `26`. Free-run outputs, full-span log-odds `D`, and the three selected pairs are completely unaffected; downstream consumers must read `guarded_greedy_argmax_token_id` and `guarded_tie_at_top1` rather than treating `guarded_top5[0]` as `argmax` on tied steps.
2. **Single-observation local 2-window timing limitations (`n = 1` per switch arm):** Each Stage A1 configuration ran once on `window-019` and once on `window-037` (`6` pages out of `60`) without AB/BA interleaving. Sub-second differences (`S0`) are within single-run host/GPU jitter, `add_S1a` exhibited a one-time cold/workspace slowdown (`+14.41 s` ordinary sum; `121.42 ms/tok` on `019` probe pass 1 vs `107.54 ms/tok` on pass 2) whereas `remove_S1a` differed from `all_on` by only `-0.72 s`, and short 3-page sequence lengths (`~1,300–1,700` total tokens) do not exercise long-context KV growth where `S2` (`StaticDashCache` + 512-key gather) was designed to pay off. Two-window marginal deltas are conditional on the other five switches, non-additive, and not 60-page forecasts.
3. **Cumulative vs. per-window counter semantics on unmodified genuine `CarbonEngine`:** Unmodified `WP-0015/carbon/engine.py:CarbonEngine` reports process-cumulative `prefill_grouped_mm_layer_calls` (`44` after 3 warmups + `019`, `55` after `037`), whereas `AblationEngine` reports per-window `SwitchActivationCounters.delta` (`11` MoE layers per window) alongside `switch_counter_cumulative`.
