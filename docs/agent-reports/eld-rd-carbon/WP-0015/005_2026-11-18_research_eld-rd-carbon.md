### 2. What Caused Evidence-Backed Gains vs What Falsified Turn 1's Sub-Component Hypothesis

- **Evidence-backed gains (`~10%–15%` across `dev/dense` and reviewer-replicated `H`, `-78.9 MiB` allocated VRAM):**
  1. *Packed BF16 MoE (`pack_moe_layer_inplace` + `pack_dense_mlp_inplace`):* On Blackwell `sm_120`, `torch._grouped_mm` succeeded natively across all `11` MoE layers during prefill (`22/22` calls per segment, `0` fallbacks, prefill `0.338–0.374 s -> 0.310–0.311 s`), freed `82,706,432 B` (`78.87 MiB`) of peak CUDA allocated memory after a `539–555 ms` in-place pack, and collapsed `198` per-token Python expert GEMV launches (`moe_python_expert_loop = 14.342 ms/step` in `native_profile`) into `22` batched `torch.bmm` launches.
  2. *`no_text_streamer`:* Removed `0.144 ms/step` (`52.30 ms` over `363` steps) of strictly post-`argmax` GPU-idle host time and eliminated Windows `cp1252` console/pipe encoding crashes during `eval_mode=False` decoding.
  3. *`StaticDashCache` + `gather_sparse_decode_step`:* Executed with `0` cache reallocations and trimmed `~3–4 s` beyond `carbon_moe` on `AB` (`31.812 s` vs `35.832 s`), while `burst_stride=8` (`HARNESS-02`) cost `6` wasted post-EOS forwards (`~0.52–0.62 s`) on `dense` and `8` (`~0.75 s`) on `warm`.
- **Causal stage diagnosis (`native_profile`, `363` steps / `256` CUDA-event steps):**
  - Turn 1 hypothesized that synchronous stalls (`tokens_per_expert.cpu().numpy()`, `torch.cat`, `argmax`) caused `>97%` of decode time.   - **`attn_router` (`_dash_decode_forward`) dominated at `83.936 ms/step` host (`69.03%`) and `85.049 ms/step` GPU event (`69.95%`)** (`~75%` of uninstrumented step time) despite having **zero `.cpu()` syncs** and `9%–19%` GPU utilization. Source audit (`modeling_deepseekv2.py:338-531`, `dash_attention.py:764-869`, `_sparse_base_impl.py:1451-1490`) confirms `_dash_decode_forward` dispatches **`~180–200` tiny eager Python/ATen/WDDM kernels per layer (`~2,200` ops/token across 12 layers: 2D page RoPE, `_RMSNorm`, `head_cls` block summaries, `1.5-entmax` sort/cumsum/thresholding, and `_decode_attend`)**.
