### 3. Root-cause mechanism of the Packet A G0 hard failure (non-key token telemetry inspection)
Without accessing scoring keys or re-adjudicating truth, read-only inspection of the frozen `g0-ab` token ID streams (`05-a-native` vs. `05-a-carbon_bf16`) explains why `carbon_bf16` was **100% deterministic across `AB`/`BA`** (`prediction_sha256` `204af33db5ba6105d4cf7c020c0c3f2c3bc456598f7840dac445c1151f0a03b1` in both orders vs. `native` `cd009c42f9e9518ac5c955b39a1b837ee4060a9dd6b81a3179a8523a88da4095` in both orders), improved overall CER (`-0.00158712`) and cell accuracy (`+0.00254345`), yet failed `newly_unflagged_critical_errors == 0` on `page/21/line/2` and `page/37/line/2`:

1. **BF16 GEMM reduction-order perturbation (~1 ULP):** `carbon_bf16` replaces `native`'s per-expert `F.linear` loop, separate `gate_proj`/`up_proj` GEMMs, and full-KV masked `_decode_attend` with `torch._grouped_mm` (prefill), concatenated `[gate_proj; up_proj]` matrices, `torch.bmm` (decode MoE), and 512-key `gather_sparse_decode_step`. In BF16 (7-bit mantissa), these algebraically equivalent rearrangements alter tensor-core tile accumulation rounding. Across `A`'s 20 windows, 1 window (`window-031`, pages 31–33) was bit-for-bit token-identical, `13/60` pages were token-identical, and `35/60` pages had identical visible text outside bounding-box coordinates (`7b70d674`).
2. **Knife-edge homoglyph flips on `page/21` and `page/37`:**
   - **Page 21 (`window-019`, pages 19–21):** The first `417` tokens of the window (all of pages 19–20 and the first 37 tokens of page 21) were **bit-for-bit identical** between `native` and `carbon_bf16`. At page-21 token indices `37..38` (prose content after `<FORK>`), `native` selected BPE tokens `[342 (' I'), 3019 ('08')]` (`" I08"`), whereas `carbon_bf16` selected `[223 (' '), 6924 ('108')]` (`" 108"`)—a single **`I` vs. `1` optical homoglyph flip** (`3` remaining token diffs on page 21 were `+-1` box-coordinate units: `235->234`, `292->291`, `259->260`).
   - **Page 37 (`window-037`, pages 37–39):** After a 1-unit coordinate rounding diff at token `7` (`17979 ('291')` vs. `16234 ('290')`), at page-37 content token indices `41..42`, `native` selected `[26 ('8'), 90169 ('OI')]` (`"8OI"`), whereas `carbon_bf16` selected `[1892 ('80'), 43 ('I')]` (`"80I"`)—a single **`O` vs. `0` optical homoglyph flip** (`6` other diffs on page 37 were box-coordinate digits).
3. **Why G1 grammar alone cannot prevent this failure, and why G0 speed was `1.11x`–`1.20x` on A:**
   - Both critical regressions occurred inside **valid post-`<FORK>` prose tokens** (`I`/`1` and `O`/`0`), while `formats.native_page` unconditionally emits `flagged: false` on every span. Even a perfect structural `<BLOCK>...<FORK>` grammar (needed for the `26/64` measured pages that exhibited `31`–`32` `title<LEVELn>` and `30` malformed `<BLOCK>` headers in `G0-stage1.json`) leaves post-`<FORK>` homoglyph ties unconstrained.
   - On 60-page `A`, total output was only `9,207`–`9,241` tokens across `20` windows (`~460` tokens/window, `~153` tokens/page). Serial `B=1` 3-page windowing therefore spent a much larger fraction of wall time in vision/SAM/CLIP prefill, PDFium rendering, and synchronous per-file write-through `fsync` (`MoveFileExW(..., 8)`) than dense table pages, limiting single-stream `carbon_bf16` speedup on `A` to `1.106x` (AB) and `1.195x` (BA).

### 4. Exact forward scope (contingent on a separate future release; nothing activated here)
With G1 and G2 rightly withheld for candidate `c7b2ee3663317e42a0e9bdd7ff9e17f16747d2cf`, any justified follow-on packet requires a **new coordinator release** addressing two orthogonal findings from G0:
1. **Eliminating or explicitly flagging BF16 homoglyph tie flips (`I`/`1`, `O`/`0`):**
   - *Path 1 (exactness-preserving execution):* Isolate which `carbon_bf16` kernel substitution (`packed_moe` `grouped_mm`/`bmm`, concatenated `[gate_proj; up_proj]`, or `gather_sparse_decode_step`) perturbed logits on `page/21` and `page/37`, or run G1 device grammar (`WP-0016 / 001` `367`-state table) directly on unmodified-math `native` GEMMs first before introducing any non-associative GEMM fusion.
   - *Path 2 (margin / cross-recognizer disagreement flagging):* Because unmodified BF16 itself sits on knife-edge `I`/`1` and `O`/`0` logits (as also seen in `A`'s 1 repair and 1 changed error in the opposite direction, and `warm-1` vs. `warm-2` cuBLAS workspace tie-breaking), emitting `flagged: true` when top-1 vs. top-2 logit margin on alphanumeric identifier tokens falls below a calibrated threshold—or when frozen Windows OCR (`15.6546455` s on `A`) disagrees on a critical token, consistent with `assignment.md` (*"Windows geometry may only flag disagreement or invite wider inspection"*)—converts silent homoglyph flips into explicit human-review flags rather than unflagged critical errors.
2. **Recorded WP-0017 proposals (unreleased, per `assignment.md`):**
   - **Self-speculation vs. asymmetric FP8:** G0's empirical result directly informs future WP-0017 prioritization. **Asymmetric FP8** introduces larger weight/activation quantization perturbations than BF16 GEMM reassociation and is therefore high-risk under an unflagged zero-critical-regression gate unless paired with calibrated uncertainty/disagreement flagging. Conversely, **greedy self-speculation** (drafting multiple tokens and verifying in parallel against the exact target BF16 forward pass) can preserve exact target greedy token selection when verification uses identical target operators.
