## Decisions and rationale

### 1. Static source audit of `locks.py` (`41d6204648e4115ab197507d1a22f6d5c7f3e784`)

- **Precedence-aware boolean AST (`locks.py:333-416`):** `_marker_norm` parses `or` (`parse_or`) -> `and` (`parse_and`) -> `cmp` / parenthesized sub-expression (`parse_expr`, bounded by `_MAX_MARKER_DEPTH = 64`). Associative `and` chains and associative `or` chains flatten in exact left-to-right order (never sorting or deduplicating clauses), singleton `(expr)` unwraps cleanly, and `fmt` re-emits parentheses strictly when a child of `and` is an `or` node (`f"({fmt(c)})" if c[0] == "or" else fmt(c)`). Consequently, all `5` observed `COORD-Q0-02` singleton-parenthesis edges (`fsspec`, `psutil` ×2, `urllib3` ×2) canonicalize identically between wheel `METADATA` and PyPI JSON `graph_requires`, whereas real precedence changes such as `(a or b) and c` vs `a or (b and c)` remain strictly unequal, and `_marker_norm` is idempotent across all `1,584` real marker strings.
- **Injective string-literal & operator tokenization (`locks.py:277-330, 405-406`):** `_tokenize_marker` scans single- and double-quoted PEP 508 literals verbatim (forbidding `\r`/`\n` and unterminated quotes), matches longest-first comparison operators (`===`, `==`, `!=`, `<=`, `>=`, `~=`, `<`, `>`), and requires whitespace between `not` and `in` (rejecting bare `not`, `notin`, or `not(...)`). `fmt_var` formats `IDENT` verbatim and `STR` via `json.dumps(tok[1], ensure_ascii=False)`, which is both injective across embedded quotes/spaces/backslashes/case and disjoint from `IDENT` (`"os_name" == "nt"` $\neq$ `os_name == "nt"`).
- **Specifier/extra validation & balanced outer specifier parentheses (`locks.py:260-267, 424-463`):** `_SPEC_RE`, `_EXTRA_NAME`, and `_VER_CLAUSE` validate distribution names, extras, and PEP 440 version clauses individually without stripping internal whitespace inside broken tokens (`my pkg>=1.0`, `pkg[a b]`, `pkg>=1. 0` all fail closed to `"!unparsed:" + req`). Balanced outer specifier parentheses (`while ver_raw.startswith("(") and ver_raw.endswith(")")` followed by rejecting empty `()` or any remaining `(` / `)`) unwrap `torch (==2.13.0)` and `pytest ((>=4.6))` while mathematically rejecting unpaired or multi-group forms (`(==1.0)(==2.0)` strips to `==1.0)(==2.0`, which still contains `)` and `(`, failing closed).
- **Two-sided `!unparsed:` guard (`locks.py:160, 172-173, 474-480`):** Both `verify_wheel_metadata` (`requires_match = got == want and not unparsed`) and `check_local_record` (`not req_target.startswith("!unparsed:")`) explicitly reject `!unparsed:` entries even if identical malformed strings appeared on both sides, while preserving full `Counter` multiset matching across all `850` active and inactive `Requires-Dist` edges and PEP 658 `published_metadata_sha256` byte hashing.
