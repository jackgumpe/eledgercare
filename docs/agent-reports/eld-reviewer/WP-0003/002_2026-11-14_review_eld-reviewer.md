2. **Disposition of Flagged Question 1 — PostgreSQL `18.7-1` Server-Subset Provenance vs Missing Whole-Archive SHA-256**:
   - **Assessment**: **Sufficient and accepted for scoped local-only F1 development/test setup.**
   - **Evidence**: `pgsql/pgAdmin 4/` accounts for `81.74%` (`318,396,799` compressed bytes) of EDB's `389,515,503`-byte Windows archive, and EDB publishes no checksum on the download page. `eld-implementer`'s `deploy/dev/postgres/fetch_server_subset.py` enforces HTTPS TLS, `206 Partial Content` span-length checks, central-directory vs local-header name/method/flag/size/CRC-32 matching (`lines 118-133`), unencrypted Stored/Deflate methods, and `os.path.normpath` + `dest_root + os.sep` path-traversal confinement (`lines 136-138`). Independent verification confirmed: (a) 1,597-for-1,597 exact `sha256`/`crc32`/`bytes`/`path` match against `postgresql-18.7-1-server-subset.sha256` (manifest SHA-256 `47c3deb9c917f79c353b3683b2c2611ee975285ea06226c8f72444c96ce9051f`); (b) live HTTPS `HEAD` and full `2,636,034`-byte central-directory download (`22,284` entries, SHA-256 `3132ca63b424f7ec371eabe50753ad7cd036278d942a6ea465547b17bf90a149`) with `0` mismatches plus live Range spot-fetches of `bin/pg_ctl.exe` and `bin/initdb.exe`; (c) `161 / 161` `.exe`/`.dll` files carrying valid EnterpriseDB Corporation Authenticode signatures (thumbprint `7BEDD1269FCCF7A5D95F18274750B79893C06C70`); and (d) runtime `--version` reporting `(PostgreSQL) 18.7`.
   - **Explicit Limits & Minor Code Note**: Whole-archive SHA-256 remains uncomputed and cannot verify whole-ZIP mirrors; non-PE files (`share/**`, `.sql`, `.a`/`.lib`) rely on HTTPS + central-directory/manifest SHA-256 rather than Authenticode. Also, `fetch_server_subset.py:93,135` selects root files via `name.count("/") == 1 and not name.endswith("/")` before slicing `entry["name"][len("pgsql/"):]`; live central-directory inspection proved `non_pgsql_entries = []` on this archive, though guarding with `name.startswith("pgsql/")` is advisable if reused on future archives.

3. **Disposition of Flagged Question 2 — Added `startxref`-Target Precondition (`PdfPigAdmission.cs:111-145`)**:
   - **Assessment**: **Sound and accepted; negligible false-rejection risk on the accepted F1 PDF 1.0–1.7 profile.**
   - **Rationale & Evidence**: Because `PdfPig 0.1.16` invokes `XrefBruteForcer` even when `UseLenientParsing = false`, corrupt `startxref` trailers (`startxref 0` or out-of-range offsets) were otherwise recovered. `StartXrefAddressesCrossReference` requires the final `startxref` offset in the trailing 2 KiB window to begin with `"xref"` + delimiter/whitespace or `<int> <ws> <int> <ws> obj` + delimiter/whitespace. Under ISO 32000-1:2008 (§7.5.4, §7.5.8, Annex F), every conforming PDF 1.0–1.7 cross-reference table, cross-reference stream, incremental update, and linearized first-page xref satisfies one of these two productions, and the 10-digit offset parser (`ReadPositiveInt`) far exceeds the `10,485,760`-byte F1 size limit. Reviewer probes (`ReviewerProbes`) empirically confirmed admission of a valid PDF 1.5 Flate-compressed `/Type /XRef` stream (`620` bytes), a PDF 1.4 incremental update (`1,047` bytes), and trailing PDF whitespace after `%%EOF`, while rejecting `startxref 0` (`InvalidCrossReference`).

4. **Disposition of Flagged Question 3 — Known `PdfPig` Recovery Limitation & Malformed-PDF Claim Precision**:
   - **Assessment**: **Accepted as accurately bounded within accepted design scope (`docs/design/F1-durable-intake.md:40`).**
   - **Technical Precision**: Two narrow cross-reference corruptions remain recoverable by `PdfPig`'s `XrefBruteForcer` and admitted: (a) shifted per-object byte offsets inside a classic `xref` table behind a valid `startxref` (explicitly pinned by `PdfPigAdmissionTests.cs:210-226`), and (b) a `startxref` offset pointing to an ordinary non-`/XRef` `<N> <G> obj` indirect object header such as `1 0 obj` (demonstrated by reviewer probe), alongside unverified tokens between the `startxref` integer and `%%EOF` prior to `PdfDocument.Open`. Both `docs/product/F1-durable-intake-implementation.md:31-38` and `WP-0003 / 001` (`Decisions 2–3`, `Risks`) state these parser limits transparently without overclaiming full PDF conformance validation, avoiding premature custom PDF parser complexity in F1.
