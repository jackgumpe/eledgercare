## Browser, persistence and deployment limits

The stock click-to-frame result was **52.604 s**. Starting the resident worker on demand with A as its first inference took **52.68 s**. Adding a separate on-demand warmup increased this to **56.66 s**. The already-ready case saved click time but had an intake-to-frame wait of **54.597 s**, compared with **53.705 s** stock. With two nearly simultaneous extractions, both results were visible **20.429 s** after the first click when ready and **66.718 s** when clicked immediately. The second ready user spent **9.25 s** waiting for claim. The database logged all 21 queued/running/completed transitions for seven distinct attempts. Source bytes, output hashes, page counts and reopened revision/attempt links agreed for all seven. The ready A database commit preceded the browser frame by 0.774 s. [Seven independent event joins](../../../reviews/WP-0014/eld-latency-reviewer/scheduling-calculation.json)

The controlled driver deliberately waited for readiness in the ready arm. Two intake signals coalesced into one warmed model and all inference was serial. The experiment demonstrates an in-viewport animation-frame opportunity on headless Edge and commit-event time in local PostgreSQL. Absolute timestamps across clock APIs have measurement uncertainty. The diagnostic bridge gives the product supervisor an attempt-specific child with fencing while its model process lives separately. Its scoped pre-CUDA crash, busy retirement refusal and orderly completion support those specific cases. Production still requires tests for interruption during decoding, stale results, restart, timeout-based retirement and overlapping arrivals. Independent repeat of the entire scheduling matrix, an alternate SGLang runtime and a GPU kernel profile were outside this review. The coordinator found no installed WSL Linux distribution or reachable Linux Docker engine, and its Windows resolver could not satisfy the custom serving dependencies. The quoted 0.5–1.2 s and a population-level service target are unverified.
